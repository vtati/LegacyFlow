# District of Columbia Content Pack (us-dc v0)

## Jurisdiction-Specific Notes

### Wills (D.C. Code §§ 18-101 et seq.)
- **Witnesses:** 2 attesting witnesses required (§ 18-103). Must sign in testator's presence.
- **Self-proving:** DC allows self-proving wills with notarized affidavits.
- **Holographic wills:** NOT valid in DC. Must meet formal requirements.
- **Probate:** DC has a simplified probate process for small estates.

### Power of Attorney (D.C. Code §§ 22-4301 et seq. — Uniform Power of Attorney Act)
- DC adopted the Uniform Power of Attorney Act.
- **Execution:** Signed by principal, acknowledged before notary.
- **Durability:** Presumed durable.

### Advance Directives (D.C. Code §§ 7-601 et seq.)
- **Durable POA for Healthcare:** DC's primary advance directive form.
- **Witnesses:** 2 witnesses required. Witnesses cannot be the agent, healthcare provider, or employee of healthcare facility (with exceptions).
- DC also recognizes living wills and anatomical gift designations.

### Key Differences from Virginia
1. Holographic wills NOT valid in DC.
2. DC witness disqualification rules are stricter for healthcare directives.
3. DC probate is generally faster/simpler than VA for small estates.
4. DC Code Title 18 (wills) vs. VA Title 64.2.
