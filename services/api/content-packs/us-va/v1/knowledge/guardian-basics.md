# Choosing guardians

Naming a guardian tells the court who you would want to care for your minor
children if neither parent can. You can name a primary guardian and one or
more backups.

A backup guardian matters because circumstances change: your first choice may
be unable to serve when the time comes. Plans without a backup are flagged so
your attorney can discuss options with you.

Your attorney finalizes guardian nominations as part of your plan documents.
This guide is educational only and is not legal advice.
