DURABLE GENERAL POWER OF ATTORNEY
Commonwealth of Virginia
(Va. Code §§ 64.2-1600 et seq. — Uniform Power of Attorney Act)

NOTICE: This is an important legal document. It creates a durable power of attorney. You should consult an attorney before signing.

PRINCIPAL: {{principal_name}}, of {{principal_address}}, {{principal_city}}, Virginia ("Principal").

AGENT: I appoint {{agent_name}}, of {{agent_address}}, as my agent and attorney-in-fact ("Agent"). If {{agent_name}} is unable or unwilling to act, I appoint {{successor_agent_name}} as successor Agent.

DURABILITY: This power of attorney is durable and shall not be affected by my subsequent disability or incapacity, pursuant to Va. Code § 64.2-1602. It shall remain in effect until revoked by me in writing.

GRANT OF AUTHORITY: My Agent shall have full power and authority to act on my behalf with respect to the following matters, to the same extent as I could act personally:

1. REAL PROPERTY — To buy, sell, lease, manage, mortgage, and convey any interest in real property.

2. TANGIBLE PERSONAL PROPERTY — To buy, sell, lease, and manage tangible personal property.

3. STOCKS AND BONDS — To buy, sell, and manage stocks, bonds, mutual funds, and other securities.

4. COMMODITIES AND OPTIONS — To buy, sell, and trade commodities, futures, and options.

5. BANKS AND FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS — To open, close, and manage bank accounts; to endorse, deposit, and withdraw funds; to borrow money.

6. OPERATION OF ENTITY OR BUSINESS — To operate, manage, and conduct any business or entity in which I have an interest.

7. INSURANCE AND ANNUITIES — To procure, manage, and surrender insurance policies and annuities.

8. ESTATES, TRUSTS, AND BENEFICIARY INTERESTS — To represent my interests in estates, trusts, and as a beneficiary.

9. CLAIMS AND LITIGATION — To commence, prosecute, defend, and settle claims and litigation on my behalf.

10. PERSONAL AND FAMILY MAINTENANCE — To provide for the support and maintenance of myself, my spouse, and my dependents.

11. BENEFITS FROM GOVERNMENTAL PROGRAMS — To apply for and manage Social Security, Medicare, Medicaid, military, and other governmental benefits.

12. RETIREMENT PLANS — To manage individual retirement accounts, 401(k) plans, pensions, and other retirement benefits.

13. TAXES — To prepare, sign, and file tax returns; to represent me before tax authorities.

{{additional_powers}}

LIMITATIONS: My Agent shall not have the authority to:
(a) Make a will or codicil on my behalf;
(b) Make healthcare decisions on my behalf (see my Advance Medical Directive);
(c) Exercise any power I do not possess.

DUTIES OF AGENT: My Agent shall act in my best interest, in good faith, and within the scope of authority granted. My Agent shall keep accurate records of all transactions.

RELIANCE: Any third party dealing with my Agent may rely on this power of attorney pursuant to Va. Code §§ 64.2-1619 through 64.2-1622. A photocopy or electronically transmitted copy of this document shall have the same effect as the original (§ 64.2-1604(D)).

EFFECTIVE DATE: This power of attorney becomes effective immediately upon execution and shall remain effective during any subsequent disability or incapacity.
