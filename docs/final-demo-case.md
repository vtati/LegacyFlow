# Final Demo — Complex Synthetic Case: The Sharma Family

## Case Design (synthetic, for demonstration)

**Family**: Blended family with minor children, small business, rental property

- **Raj Sharma**, 45 — second marriage, owns "Sharma IT Consulting LLC" (S-corp, ~$400k annual revenue)
- **Priya Sharma**, 42 — second marriage, works as a nurse practitioner
- **Aarav Sharma**, 16 — Raj's son from first marriage (lives with them full-time)
- **Diya Sharma**, 12 — Priya's daughter from first marriage (lives with them full-time)  
- **Kabir Sharma**, 7 — Raj and Priya's son together

**Assets**:
- Primary residence: Single-family home in Fairfax, VA (~$750k, $380k mortgage)
- Rental property: Condo in Arlington, VA (~$420k, $210k mortgage, $2,400/mo rent)
- Business: Sharma IT Consulting LLC (S-corp)
- Retirement: Raj 401(k) ~$280k, Priya 403(b) ~$190k
- Life insurance: Raj $750k term, Priya $500k term

**Key planning questions**:
1. Guardianship: If both parents pass, who cares for the kids? Complex because:
   - Aarav (16) is almost an adult, but still a minor
   - Blended family — should the guardian be the same for all three?
   - Raj's brother in California vs Priya's sister in Richmond
2. Business succession: What happens to the LLC? (buy-sell? wind down?)
3. Rental property: Keep for income or sell?
4. Healthcare: Who makes medical decisions? (healthcare proxy)
5. Financial: Who manages money for minors? (trust vs custodial)

**Intentional consistency issue** (for the consistency check demo):
- Raj says the business should go to his brother (who's not involved in it)
- But also says he wants the rental income to support the kids
- Potential conflict: if the business is wound down, does the family lose income needed for the mortgage?

## Interview Flow (dry run topics)

1. Household composition (blended family)
2. Minor children + guardianship preferences
3. Business ownership + succession wishes
4. Real property (primary + rental)
5. Healthcare decision-maker
6. Financial decision-maker for minors
7. Existing documents (none — starting fresh)
8. Attorney review questions (recorded for attorney)
